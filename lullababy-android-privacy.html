2. Audio, video and sound events

Microphone and camera content may include personal information about children, adults and their surroundings. Audio processing and sound-event detection run on your phone. Video is captured only when enabled. Content is not processed by cloud recognition or used for AI training.

Direct listening sends audio to the external output selected through the operating system. Two-device monitoring sends audio, enabled video and sound events to your confirmed station over an authenticated, end-to-end encrypted local connection. The developer and RevenueCat cannot decrypt that stream. Direct Bluetooth output uses Bluetooth and operating-system protections.

LullaBaby does not save audio or video recordings or send them to developer servers, RevenueCat or a cloud relay. Temporary media buffers are overwritten or released during processing and shutdown. Sound-event types and times may appear in the session, local notifications and diagnostics.

3. Discovery and connected stations

Devices on the reachable local network may see your advertised station name, local network address and public pairing identifier. Discovery and public handshake information are not fully encrypted. Avoid private information in station names.

Confirmed stations exchange media, sound events, connection status, phone battery level and mode settings. They also exchange whether each station has its own active paid entitlement, without receipts, payment details or a full purchase profile. Access sponsored by the other station lasts only for that connection. Saved trust allows a station to recognise your installation in later sessions.

4. Local storage and diagnostics

Installation keys, trusted station identities and names, and the free allowance are stored in encrypted local files protected by Android Keystore. Your station name and preferences are stored locally. Android app-data backup is disabled.

The local diagnostic log contains technical events and timestamps and is periodically cleared when it exceeds approximately 40 KB. Current session metrics are held in memory. You can clear the history in settings.

Diagnostics contain no audio, images, station names, network addresses, keys or QR contents and are not uploaded automatically. An export is created only when requested. If you share it, your selected app or recipient controls its copy.

RevenueCat stores a technical identifier and cached purchase and entitlement information locally. Removing a local cache does not delete the server profile.

5. Purchases

Google Play handles payments. We do not receive full card numbers, banking passwords or payment details entered in the store. RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase information on our behalf to validate, restore and provide paid access and produce purchase analytics and sales reports.

RevenueCat processes an automatically generated pseudonymous App User ID, product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status and duration, entitlements, price, currency and store information available through the SDK. Requests include platform, device model, app, operating-system and SDK versions and locale. Servers receive your IP address. Pseudonymous identifiers are not legally anonymous.

The purchase SDK may initialise at launch and create a technical profile or check entitlements before you buy. We use no advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers or cross-service advertising tracking. We do not send RevenueCat nursery audio, video, station names or sound events.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy; RevenueCat Privacy Policy; RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum.

6. Permissions and background use

Microphone: live nursery audio and local sound-event detection after you start monitoring. Active audio uses a foreground service with a system notification and may continue with the screen locked or the app in the background. Microphone capture does not restart automatically after process termination.

Camera: pairing QR scanning and live video when you enable it. Child-station video pauses when the app backgrounds.

Bluetooth, nearby devices and Wi-Fi: connected external audio output and local station discovery. LullaBaby does not request location permission or use discovery to determine your location.

Network access, multicast and wake locks: local connectivity, active audio and purchase requests. Station connections require a common local Wi-Fi network or compatible hotspot.

Notifications: background audio status and sound, connection, battery and allowance alerts. Other people may see them on the lock screen.

You can revoke permissions in system app settings; affected features will stop working. Stop monitoring in LullaBaby to end current capture. Video is optional and can be disabled while retaining audio.

Recipients include your confirmed station, RevenueCat and its infrastructure processors, Google for store and system services, and recipients you select for diagnostics. If you contact our Gmail address, Google processes correspondence as the mail provider and the developer receives your message and attachments.

When this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information, including an IP address for security. The page contains no trackers, scripts, forms or third-party fonts. Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy; GitHub Privacy Statement.

Providers processing data on our behalf are required to protect it at least as described in this policy and required by law, and to process it only for agreed purposes. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Minimum necessary information may be disclosed to satisfy a valid legal demand or protect lawful rights.

Purchase, mail and hosting providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We use applicable processing agreements and EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism where required. RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum describes RevenueCat’s safeguards. You may request applicable safeguards by email. Local media is not routed through these providers.

Minimal diagnostics, security, prevention of free-allowance abuse and sales reporting rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). 9. Retention

Audio and video exist only in temporary processing buffers. Preferences and trusted-station information remain until changed, removed or deleted from the relevant local storage. Installation identity and allowance records support security and access accounting; deletion depends on the platform as explained below.

Diagnostics remain until cleared or replaced under the limits stated above. The operating system controls notification retention. Exported copies remain under their recipients’ control.

RevenueCat profiles and purchases remain while needed for access validation, restoration and related purchase analytics, or until a deletion request is fulfilled, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Uninstalling does not delete the server profile. The store retains its own records under its policy.

Support correspondence remains while needed to resolve a request; minimum related information may remain for applicable claims or mandatory retention periods. If deletion is limited by an exception, we explain its basis and retention period or criterion.

10. Control and deletion

Stop the session, revoke permissions in system settings, and clear diagnostics or remove trusted phones in LullaBaby settings while the station is stopped. Remove a trust record on both phones to remove mutual trust. We have no remote access to delete data on your phones.

Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes local app data. Uninstalling also removes the app’s Android Keystore keys. These actions do not delete Google Play or RevenueCat records.

To access, correct or delete purchase-profile data or correspondence, email kazachonaksiarhei@gmail.com with the subject “LullaBaby — personal data”. Include your platform and enough information to find the record, such as a transaction number or App User ID if known. We may request minimum information to verify ownership.

We delete data under our control and instruct our processors, subject to legally required retention or necessary protection of lawful rights. Later SDK requests or purchase restoration may recreate a technical profile. Deletion does not cancel a subscription or delete your Google account.

11. Your rights and children’s information

As applicable, you may request access, a copy, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Under GDPR we respond without undue delay and within one month; a permitted extension and its reason are communicated within that period. You may complain to Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) or your local supervisory authority.

LullaBaby is intended for adult parents and caregivers. Children’s media is processed locally and delivered to your confirmed station; we do not request a child’s name, age, birth date or profile or collect their media on a server. You must have the necessary authority and consents to monitor. Contact us if a child’s information was improperly sent to support.

12. Security and policy changes

Station connections use authentication, encryption and replay protection; installation keys use platform security. RevenueCat requests use HTTPS. No protection is absolute. Confirm trusted stations, protect pairing credentials and keep your devices updated. A recipient may use separate recording tools.

We update this policy and its date when processing changes. Material changes are communicated through an available in-app method before the relevant processing begins, with separate consent where required. Contact: kazachonaksiarhei@gmail.com.
