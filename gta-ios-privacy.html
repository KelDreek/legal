Leonida for iOS — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy describes how Leonida for iOS (com.leonida.gtaTracker) handles information in its current version.

1. Information stored on your device

Leonida stores cheat favourites, selected game and platform, onboarding completion, map positions and filters, bookmarks, discoveries, notes, personal pins, visit plans and saved presets in local databases or device preferences. Map and guide searches run locally. Fictional game coordinates are not your phone’s location.

The current app does not upload these records or search queries to the developer, RevenueCat, Rockstar Games or Take-Two. It has no Leonida login, public posting, cloud sync, cloud AI or connection to your game account, saves or player telemetry.

iOS may include ordinary databases and preferences in system backups. An upgrade from an earlier version may also leave a protected local pre-migration safety copy, excluded from system backup. That copy can retain earlier records after you edit or delete the current records; it remains until the app’s local data is removed and has no separate in-app erase control.

2. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store payments. We do not receive full payment-card numbers or banking passwords. RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase information on our behalf to verify and restore Leonida Plus, determine available offers and trial eligibility, prevent incorrect access grants, and provide purchase analytics and sales reporting.

In a configured build, the SDK starts when the app opens, including before a purchase. It creates or uses a persisted pseudonymous App User ID and sends entitlement and offering requests. Pseudonymous does not mean legally anonymous.

Data includes that identifier, product and transaction IDs, receipts or transaction information, purchase and renewal dates, subscription duration and status, expiry, entitlements, introductory-offer information, prices, currency and storefront information available through the SDK. Servers receive IP addresses and request times. The SDK stores identifiers and cached purchase information locally.

3. Technical information and purchase analytics

RevenueCat requests include device model, operating-system, app and SDK versions, locale and technical app/request state. On iOS, the SDK also sends the available identifier for this vendor (IDFV), which is different from the advertising IDFA.

If a RevenueCatUI paywall or Customer Center is displayed, its SDK may process purchase-flow interactions and technical events. RevenueCatUI paywalls record impressions, closes and offer interactions linked to the technical purchase profile, and may queue events locally pending delivery.

The current app supplies no name, email or phone number as RevenueCat customer attributes. It contains no advertising SDK or general-purpose analytics/crash-reporting integration such as Google Analytics or Firebase. This does not exclude RevenueCat purchase analytics.

4. Permissions and clipboard

The current app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, photos or physical-location access for its companion features. Maps and reference content are bundled; configured purchase services require internet.

When you choose Copy, the selected game command or in-game phone code is written to the system clipboard. The app does not read unrelated clipboard contents for this feature. iOS and other software with permitted clipboard access control further handling of copied text.

5. Support and this website

If you contact support, the developer receives your sender address, message and attachments you choose to send. Correspondence is handled through Gmail. Avoid including passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Opening an external website connects your browser to its operator, which may receive your IP address and browser information. GitHub receives technical requests when this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, including IP addresses for security. This HTML page itself has no trackers, scripts, forms or third-party fonts.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where GDPR applies, providing requested app and paid-access features, verifying/restoring purchases and handling service requests rely on performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)). Security, prevention of incorrect access grants and proportionate purchase analytics and sales reporting rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). Mandatory recordkeeping and valid legal demands rely on legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

These bases do not replace any separate consent required for non-essential analytics or storing/accessing device information. This policy and merely using the app are not that consent. You may withdraw separately given consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Automated entitlement checks control paid-feature availability; we do not make solely automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

8. Recipients and international processing

Recipients include RevenueCat and its infrastructure processors for purchases, Apple for store services, Google for support mail, GitHub for website hosting, and external sites or recipients you select. Providers acting on our behalf must process data for agreed purposes and protect it under applicable law and this policy. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Minimum necessary information may be disclosed to meet lawful obligations or protect legal rights.

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers from the EEA use applicable processing agreements and EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful mechanism where required. Ask the developer for information about applicable safeguards.

Provider documents: RevenueCat Privacy Policy; RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum; Apple Privacy Policy; Google Privacy Policy; GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Retention

Local records remain until changed, removed or deleted with the relevant app data, subject to the migration-copy and system-backup behaviour above. RevenueCat profiles, purchases and paywall events remain while needed for access validation, restoration and related purchase analytics, or until a deletion request is fulfilled, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Uninstalling alone does not delete the server profile. Apple retains its own records under its policy.

Support correspondence remains while needed to resolve a request; minimum related information may remain for applicable claims or mandatory retention periods. If deletion is limited by an exception, we explain its basis and retention period or criterion.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit or remove personal records through the available app controls. Delete the app together with its data in iOS settings to remove its local databases, preferences and migration copy. Offloading keeps documents and data. Manage system backups separately; restoring a backup can restore records. We have no remote access to retrieve or erase records held only on your device.

For access, correction or deletion of purchase-profile data or support correspondence, email the developer with the subject “Leonida — personal data”, your platform and enough information to locate the record, such as a transaction number or App User ID if known. We may request minimum ownership verification. We delete data under our control and instruct processors, subject to lawful exceptions. Future SDK requests or restoration may recreate a profile. Deletion does not cancel subscriptions or delete your Apple account.

As applicable, you may request access, a copy, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw separately given consent. Under GDPR we respond without undue delay and within one month, notifying you within that period of a permitted extension and its reason. You may complain to Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) or your local supervisory authority.

11. Audience, security and changes

Leonida is not directed to children under 13. Its references concern mature fictional games; applicable store age and content restrictions also apply. We do not request children’s profiles or knowingly seek their personal information. Contact the developer if a child’s information was improperly provided.

Store and RevenueCat communications use HTTPS; local storage relies on iOS protections. No protection is absolute. Keep your device secure and updated. We update this policy and its date when processing changes, communicate material changes through an available in-app method and obtain separate consent where required before new processing begins.
