2. Map use and personal records

The bundled map, guide, catalog and search work locally. Discoveries, bookmarks, notes, custom pins, visit plans, saved filters, language and display preferences are stored on your device. Fictional map coordinates are not your phone’s geographical location.

Leonida does not upload these personal records or search queries to the developer, RevenueCat, Rockstar Games or Take-Two. There is no cloud sync, online community posting or cloud AI processing in the current app. The app does not connect to your game account, game saves or player telemetry.

The local database and preferences may be included in system backups according to your iOS settings. An upgrade from the earlier app may also keep a protected, local pre-migration safety copy. This copy is excluded from system backup and can retain earlier records after you edit or delete them in the current database; it remains until the app’s local data is removed. There is no in-app control to erase this safety copy separately.

Local records remain until you edit or delete them or remove the app’s data, subject to the backup behaviour described above. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase data stored only on your device.

3. Subscriptions and technical data

Apple App Store processes payments. We do not receive full payment-card numbers or banking passwords. RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase information on our behalf to validate and restore Leonida Plus access and provide purchase analytics and sales reporting.

When store services are configured in your build, RevenueCat starts when the app opens, including before a purchase. It creates or uses a persisted pseudonymous App User ID and can contact its servers to check access and offers. A build without configured store services does not make these SDK requests.

The data includes App User IDs, product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, purchase and renewal dates, subscription period, expiry and renewal status, entitlements, prices, currencies and store information available through the SDK. Requests include device model, operating-system, app and SDK versions, locale and technical request information. Servers receive an IP address and request times, including activity associated with access checks. Pseudonymous identifiers are not legally anonymous.

On iOS, the SDK also sends the available identifier for this vendor (IDFV, not the advertising IDFA), device model and technical app state. If a RevenueCatUI paywall is displayed, RevenueCat processes paywall impressions, closes and offer interactions linked to the technical purchase profile for purchase analytics.

RevenueCat keeps local identifiers and cached purchase information and may queue paywall events for delivery. Removing a local cache does not remove the server profile. The app does not provide your name, email or phone number as RevenueCat customer attributes. We use no advertising SDKs or cross-service advertising tracking.

Provider documents: Apple Privacy Policy; RevenueCat Privacy Policy; RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum.

4. Permissions, support and external websites

The current app does not request access to your physical location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos for its map features. Internet is used for configured store services and external links, rather than downloading live map tiles.

If you email support, the developer receives your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Google processes correspondence through Gmail. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in notes or support attachments.

Opening a source or other external link connects your browser to that website; its operator may receive your IP address and browser information. When this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information, including IP addresses for security. This page contains no trackers, scripts, forms or external fonts. Provider documents: Google Privacy Policy; GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Retention

Personal map records and migration or system backups follow the local retention rules above. RevenueCat profiles, purchase records and paywall events remain while needed for access validation, restoration and related purchase analytics, or until a deletion request is fulfilled, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Uninstalling alone does not delete the server profile. The store keeps its own records under its policy.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request; minimum related information may remain for applicable claims or mandatory retention periods. If deletion is limited by a legal exception, we explain its basis and the applicable period or criterion.

8. Deletion and your rights

Delete the app together with its data in iOS settings to remove its current local database, preferences and migration safety copy. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. System backups are managed separately in your Apple settings; restoring a backup can restore local records.

To request access, a copy, correction or deletion of purchase-profile data or support correspondence, email kazachonaksiarhei@gmail.com with the subject “Leonida — personal data”. Include your platform and enough information to locate the record, such as a transaction number or App User ID if known. We may request minimum information to verify ownership; do not send passwords or payment-card details.

We delete data under our control and instruct our processors, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Future SDK requests or restoration may recreate a technical profile. Data deletion does not cancel a subscription or delete your store account.

As applicable, you may request restriction and portability, object to legitimate-interest processing and withdraw separately given consent. Under GDPR, we respond without undue delay and within one month, notifying you within that period of a permitted extension and its reason. You may complain to Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) or your local supervisory authority.

9. Audience, security and changes

Leonida is not directed to children under 13. Its guide references a mature fictional game, and store age and content restrictions also apply. We do not request children’s profiles or knowingly seek personal data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was improperly provided.

Store and RevenueCat communications use HTTPS. Local storage relies on operating-system protections; no security measure is absolute. Protect your device and keep it updated.
